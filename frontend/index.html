What we do

Three pillars that shape every gathering

Everything we run — from Sunday fellowships to weekday outreach — sits under one of these.

Formation

Spiritual Formation

Bible study, choir, prayer cells and retreats that ground members in scripture and sacrament.

Outreach

Community Service

Hospital visits, environmental clean-ups and support for vulnerable families across the diocese.

Growth

Talent & Skills

Mentorship, career talks and hands-on training so members grow spiritually and practically.

Why register

Membership keeps you in the loop — and gives you a voice

Registered members receive a membership number, get priority on retreats and trips, can vote at AGMs, and access welfare support in times of need.

Welfare contribution: KSh 50 per month. This supports member care and emergency assistance.

  • ✓ A unique, verifiable membership number
  • ✓ Access to welfare & emergency support fund
  • ✓ Voting rights at Annual General Meetings
  • ✓ Priority booking for retreats, camps & trips
