About Us

Growing young people in faith, character and service

Manyatta Kisumu Diocese Youths is the official youth ministry serving young people across parishes in Kisumu Diocese — open to teens and young adults who want to grow spiritually while making real friendships.

Our Mission

Mission

To build spiritual growth, unity, leadership, and social empowerment among the youths.

Our Vision

Vision

To raise a generation of youths rooted in Christ and equipped for service.

Our Foundation

Established under Nomiya Church, Kisumu Diocese

We, the Manyatta Kisumu Diocese Youths, being young people united in Christ, do hereby establish this constitution to guide our fellowship, leadership, membership, welfare, discipline, and service to God and community.

The group operates under the authority of the church leadership, with a written constitution setting out our membership, leadership structure, welfare and discipline guidelines.

Core Values

  • Faith in God
  • Integrity and Accountability
  • Unity and Cooperation
  • Discipline and Respect
  • Service and Sacrifice
  • Transparency and Responsibility
Our Objectives

What we're working towards

To nurture spiritual growth through fellowship, prayers, and teachings.

To promote unity, discipline, and service within the church and community.

To support members socially, financially, and spiritually.

To develop leadership and talents for church and community service.

To organize programs and activities that empower youths morally, spiritually, and economically.

Governed by a clear constitution

Our structure, membership rules and subscription guidelines are all documented and available to every member.

Welfare contribution: KSh 50 per month.
